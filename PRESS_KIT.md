## Short project description

MalGuard is an open-source defensive security project focused on helping people inspect files and gaming modifications with evidence rather than assumptions. GTA Guard is its GTA V mod-security product focus.

## GTA Guard at a glance

- **Audience:** People who want to understand potential risks in supported GTA V mod files before installing them.
- **Platform:** Windows x64 preview; check the official release-status page for current availability and requirements.
- **Approach:** Static inspection of supported formats, visible findings, and explicit uncertainty when required evidence is missing.
- **Website demo:** Illustrative, fictional samples only. It does not scan, upload or execute real visitor files.
- **Limits:** No scanner can guarantee that a file is harmless. Static analysis and isolation capabilities are not equivalent. Unsupported or incomplete checks must not be represented as proof of safety.
- **Independence:** MalGuard is an independent project, not an official Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive product.

## Editorial and advertising guidelines

1. Link to `https://malguard.site/` as the official website; avoid third-party installer mirrors.
2. Do not claim 100% detection, independent benchmark rankings, verified malware blocking, or full sandbox isolation without published supporting tests.
3. Distinguish the site's scripted demonstration from the downloadable Windows application.
4. Verify the latest release, signature, hashes, and download availability directly on the release-status page before publishing a claim.
5. Do not imply endorsement by Rockstar Games or any unrelated platform.
6. Use approved original MalGuard artwork. Project assets are in https://github.com/MalGuard/MalGuard/tree/main/assets; check asset licensing before third-party reuse.
