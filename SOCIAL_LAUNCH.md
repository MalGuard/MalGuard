The browser demo uses fictional files and does not analyze visitors' real mods.

## LinkedIn (professional, English)

Gaming mods often arrive as archives, scripts, and Windows plugins, and file names do not establish trust.

We're building **MalGuard**, an independent open-source defensive security project. GTA Guard focuses on showing static evidence for supported GTA V mods while being transparent about unknown or incomplete results.

Our site offers a clearly labeled educational demo and product documentation. We don't claim that a clean scan guarantees safety, and we distinguish static inspection from execution isolation.
